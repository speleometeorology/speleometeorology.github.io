Understanding the Variability in Gas Concentrations of Carlsbad Cavern

Long-term observations of subterranean atmospheric processes

← Back to main site

Project Overview

This project focuses on long-term monitoring of cave air temperature, humidity, airflow, and trace gases to understand ventilation dynamics and surface–subsurface atmospheric coupling.

Methods

  • Distributed temperature and relative humidity sensors
  • Low-power CO₂ and radon monitoring
  • Autonomous datalogging systems
  • Surface meteorological station coupling

Current Status

Instruments were deployed in multiple cave environments and data collection is complete. Preliminary analysis focuses on seasonal ventilation regimes.
