Game Description

Test your strength and endurance in this rope-climbing challenge! Choose a climber avatar and race to ascend a 225-foot rope by pressing the space bar or tapping the screen as fast as you can.

Your goal is to reach the top in the shortest possible time. Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

Background: Spirit World is located 260 feet above the floor of the Big Room in Carlsbad Cavern of Carlsbad Caverns National Park. For my Carlsbad Cavern research, I had to ascend a 225 foot rope with a 55 lb lead acid battery once a month to power my speleometeorology station placed there. All climbers in this game have ascended the rope with the battery.
